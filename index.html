Venue Locations

Easily navigate to Vijaya Mahal in Chinnalapatti and GVN Mahal in Thirukazhukundram

Engagement & Wedding Venue

Vijaya Mahal (விஜயா மஹால்)

Chinnalapatti, Dindigul District, Tamil Nadu. Renowned for its spacious halls and traditional celebratory ambiance, located conveniently off the Madurai-Dindigul highway.

Nearest Station: Dindigul Junction (~15 mins drive)

Nearest Airport: Madurai International Airport (~55 mins)

Parking: Ample dedicated parking space available

Open in Google Maps
Reception & Virunthu Venue

GVN Mahal (ஜிவிஎன் மஹால்)

Thirukazhukundram, Chengalpattu District, Tamil Nadu. A grand air-conditioned banquet hall situated in the historic temple town near Mahabalipuram & Chengalpattu.

Nearest Station: Chengalpattu Junction (~20 mins drive)

Highway: Easy access from ECR and OMR corridors

Parking: Dedicated valet and visitor parking on-site
